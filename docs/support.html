Support policy

  • Support is provided by email for App Store customers and users of WPSApp.
  • Please include your iOS version, device model, and a short description of the issue (screenshots welcome).
  • We provide guidance for using the App on networks you own or administer.
  • We do not provide assistance for unauthorized network access or misuse of diagnostic tools.
  • Feature requests and bug reports are welcome and help improve the App.
  • Support does not include custom development, on-site IT services, or router vendor warranty claims.

Common topics

SSID not showing
Connect to Wi‑Fi (not cellular only), allow Location for WPSApp, and try on a real iPhone. The Simulator often cannot read Wi‑Fi names.
Device scan
Allow Local Network access. Scanning uses TCP probes on your LAN; results depend on your network and device firewalls.
Router setup tips
iOS cannot read Wi‑Fi Protected Setup status from the access point. Use Router risk snapshot / Security checklist and your router admin page for setup guidance on networks you own.
Lookups & speed test
These tools need Internet access and call public endpoints you initiate from the App.
