Privacy Policy

WPSApp · WiFi Analyzer & Scanner

Effective date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WPSApp (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the iOS application. Contact: impro19dev@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

WPSApp is a Wi‑Fi scanning and network analysis app. It helps you review networks you own or administer (for example device discovery, ping, DNS, speed estimate, and router setup checklists). It does not recover Wi‑Fi passwords or provide tools for unauthorized access.

2. Information the App may access

Depending on features you use and permissions you grant, the App may access:

  • Location (When In Use) — required by iOS to read the current Wi‑Fi network name (SSID) and related network identifiers. We do not use location to track your physical movements for advertising.
  • Local Network — to discover reachable devices on networks you own or administer, and to run LAN diagnostics you initiate.
  • Network information — such as IP address, gateway, subnet, and BSSID, processed on device for diagnostics.
  • Device storage (preferences) — checklist progress and similar settings may be saved locally via Shared Preferences / UserDefaults.
  • Diagnostics you request — when you run tools (for example DNS lookup, HTTP headers, Whois/RDAP, MAC vendor lookup, speed test), the App sends network requests to the destinations you choose or to public lookup/speed endpoints.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party ad trackers in the App.
  • We do not collect precise continuous location for marketing.

4. How information is used

  • To show your current Wi‑Fi/LAN details and diagnostic results in the App.
  • To perform user-initiated network utilities.
  • To remember optional checklist/settings on your device.
  • To respond if you contact support by email.

5. Third-party services & SDKs

The App uses standard Flutter/iOS libraries and may contact public services only when you use related tools:

  • network_info_plus / connectivity_plus — network status and Wi‑Fi details on device.
  • permission_handler — requests OS permissions.
  • shared_preferences — local settings storage on device.
  • url_launcher — opens links (for example your router admin page) in Safari/system browser.
  • http — user-initiated requests (speed test sample download, HTTP headers, MAC vendor APIs, RDAP/Whois lookups).
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — displays banner advertisements. Google and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Those third-party endpoints may process the IP address of your request and the query you submit (domain, URL, or MAC). Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

5b. Advertising

The App shows ads via Google AdMob using non-personalized ad requests. We do not use App Tracking Transparency or request permission to track you across apps and websites. Ad serving may still use limited technical data (such as IP address) as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data retention

Diagnostic results are generally shown in memory for the session and are not uploaded to our servers. Local checklist/settings remain on your device until you delete the App or clear App data. Emails you send to support are retained as needed to respond and for ordinary business records.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information via support email, contact us and we will delete it where appropriate.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate for an on-device utility App. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. Use the App only on networks you own or are authorized to test.

9. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke Location / Local Network permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Avoid running lookup or speed-test tools if you do not want those network requests.
  • Uninstall the App to remove local preferences stored by the App.

10. International users

Processing primarily occurs on your device. When you contact us or use online lookup tools, data may be processed in other countries by email providers or those third-party services.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy.
